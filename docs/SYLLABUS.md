# Discrete Mathematics Syllabus

## MODULE I (9 hours): Principles of Inclusion and Exclusion
Introduction to Sets, Relation and Functions, Basics of Counting: Sum and product rules. Combination with repetitions. The pigeonhole principle. The principle of Inclusion-Exclusion, The Generalizations of the Principles of Inclusion-Exclusion, Derangements: Nothing is in its Right Place, Rook Polynomials, Arrangements with Forbidden Positions.

## MODULE II (9 hours): Mathematical Logic
Logic and Propositions, Tautology and Contradiction, Propositional equivalences, Laws of Logic, Conditional statements, Rules of Inference, Predicates and Quantifiers.

## MODULE III (9 hours): Generating Functions and Recurrence Relation
Ordinary Generating Functions: Introductory Examples, Definition and Examples, Counting Technique, Partitions of Integers, Exponential Generating Functions. The First-Order Linear Recurrence Relation, The Second-Order Linear Homogeneous Recurrence Relations with Constant Coefficients, Non-Homogeneous Recurrence Relations.

## MODULE IV (9 hours): Introduction to Graph Theory
Definitions and examples of Graphs, vertex degree and Handshaking Property, Isomorphism, subgraphs and complement of the subgraphs, Walks and their classification, Connected and disconnected graph, Euler Trails and Circuits, Hamilton Paths and Cycles, Planar graphs, Euler's formula, Detection of planarity by elementary reduction, dual of a planar graph, Coloring and Chromatic polynomials.

## MODULE V (9 hours): Trees and Algorithms
Trees and their Basic properties, spanning trees, Rooted Trees, Sorting, Prefix Codes, Optimal Prefix code and Weighted Trees, Minimal Spanning Trees: Kruskal's and Prim's Algorithms, Dijkstra's Shortest Path Algorithm.
